{{AI agents · ACP + MCP + OKF}}

{{Your agent sees, walks and edits your architecture.}}

{{A side panel (Alt+2, pinned or floating) to chat with Claude, Google Antigravity, GitHub Copilot or any ACP-compatible agent, with access to your vault through MCP and OKF indexes.}}

{{It does not just read code: it understands the whole context}}

{{With index.md (OKF v0.3) it walks the structure without spending tokens; with render_diagram it sees the rendered diagrams; and it edits with patch_note and expected_hash to avoid overwrites.}}
